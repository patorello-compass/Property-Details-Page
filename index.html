Listing Detail Page

Four versions of the Compass LDP rebuild. The hero section is the part built for iteration; everything below it is deliberately lighter-weight.

Shared by all four: a 1100px content container, containerized content cards on an accent surface, and the Compass DS token layer — real tokens, self-hosted Compass typefaces and cx-icons.

Hero slider heroSlider.html The default page. A 15-photo carousel that matches production's item count at every breakpoint but runs full-bleed instead of capped: one big photo at roughly half the band, then the grid, with the last two pages as two 50% photos. The band never grows past 450px tall, so the crop steps between slide counts rather than stretching. Hero slider, max width heroSliderMaxWidth.html The same page with the gallery capped at 2600px and centred, the way production caps its own gallery while the page keeps growing around it. Past the cap the slide count, band height and crops all freeze, and the gallery simply gains white space either side. Single image single-image.html A one-photo listing, so no carousel at all — its own component, unrelated to the slider. The photo spans the container's full content box at 450px tall and crops to fill it, with no mat. Portrait portrait.html The same single-photo hero with a 2:3 source. A portrait can't be cropped to that wide a window without losing the subject, so it runs at its own ratio, centred on a Neutral 30 mat that fills the rest of the frame.
